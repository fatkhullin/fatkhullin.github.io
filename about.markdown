I am an incoming Assistant Professor in the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.lse.ac.uk/mathematics/people) at the [London School of Economics and Political Science](https://www.lse.ac.uk/) (LSE), starting in 2027. Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the [Department of Management Science and Engineering](https://msande.stanford.edu/) at [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu/), mentored by [Prof. Renyuan Xu](https://profiles.stanford.edu/renyuan-xu) and [Prof. Madeleine Udell](https://profiles.stanford.edu/madeleine-udell).

I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), advised by [Prof. Niao He](https://odi.inf.ethz.ch/niaohe).

My research develops the mathematical foundations and algorithms for reliable, efficient, and scalable learning. Spanning optimization, machine learning, and reinforcement learning, I connect rigorous theory with algorithm design to advance learning and decision-making in complex environments.

My work has appeared in NeurIPS, ICML, AISTATS, JMLR, and SIAM journals in optimization and control. My research has been supported by an SNSF Postdoc.Mobility Fellowship and an [ETH AI Center Doctoral Fellowship](https://ai.ethz.ch/research/phd-and-postdoc-programs/phd-fellowships.html).
