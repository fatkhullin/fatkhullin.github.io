I am an incoming Assistant Professor in the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.lse.ac.uk/Mathematics/Research/Optimisation-and-Algorithms) at the [London School of Economics and Political Science](https://www.lse.ac.uk/) (LSE), starting in 2027. I will be recruiting Ph.D. students for Fall 2027.

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the [Department of Management Science and Engineering](https://msande.stanford.edu/) at [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu/), mentored by [Prof. Renyuan Xu](https://profiles.stanford.edu/renyuan-xu) and [Prof. Madeleine Udell](https://profiles.stanford.edu/madeleine-udell). I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html) in September 2026, where I was an ETH AI Center Fellow advised by [Prof. Niao He](https://odi.inf.ethz.ch/niaohe). Previously, I worked with [Prof. Boris Polyak](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Zhlib28AAAAJ&hl=en) on control theory, [Prof. Guanghui (George) Lan](https://www.isye.gatech.edu/users/guanghui-lan) on functional constrained optimization, and [Prof. Peter Richtárik](https://richtarik.org) on communication-efficient distributed training.

My research develops theoretically grounded algorithms for machine learning and optimization, with an emphasis on data efficiency, scalability, and safety.

My postdoctoral research at Stanford is supported by a competitive Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) Postdoc.Mobility Fellowship. I previously received the [ETH AI Center Doctoral Fellowship](https://ai.ethz.ch/research/phd-and-postdoc-programs/phd-fellowships.html) for my Ph.D. studies in Switzerland and a [DAAD Scholarship](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?detail=50026200) for my master's studies in Germany.
